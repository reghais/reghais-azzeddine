About Me

I hold a PhD in Hydrogeology from Jijel University, Algeria. My academic journey is strongly grounded in sub-surface water sciences and ground mechanical stability.

My primary research interests lie at the intersection of modern technology and geoscience. Currently, I am deeply focused on applying Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and Machine Learning (ML) models to tackle environmental and hydrological challenges. My recent works explore:

  • Compositional Data Analysis (CoDA) for advanced hydrochemical modeling.
  • Remote Sensing (RS) data integration in arid and semi-arid terrain assessments.
  • Predictive Modeling of water resources contamination and geochemical evolution.
  • Slope Stability & Landslide Analysis for infrastructure hazard reduction.

Proficient in data-science tools including R and Python, I strive to advance scientific methodologies, deliver robust water resource assessments, and contribute actively to global water security and geological hazard mitigation.

Research & Methodological Focus

Hydrogeology & Water Chemistry

Studying groundwater circulation, aquifer vulnerability, pollution hazard mapping, and hydrogeochemical mineralisation processes.

Compositional Data Analysis (CoDA)

Using log-ratio transformations to accurately model geochemical signatures and close-sum relative datasets in water and soils.

Machine Learning & Data Science

Applying advanced algorithms (Random Forest, SVM, PCA) in Python and R for predictive environmental modeling and classification.

GIS & Remote Sensing (RS)

Integrating spatial analysis, satellite imagery, morphometrics, and DEM models to explore basins and soil conservation priority zones.

Geotechnical & Slope Stability

Analyzing mechanical stresses in geological slopes, studying landslide triggers, and supporting safe civil infrastructure design.

Soil & Water Conservation

Developing multidisciplinary frameworks to mitigate erosion, manage runoff, and sustain agricultural water demands in arid basins.
